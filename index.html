Help Centre
How do I place an order?
Browse products, tap any item, then press "Shop Now". Select your size/colour, fill in your delivery details, and choose Pay After Delivery or Order via WhatsApp.
How do I track my order?
Go to Menu → Orders to see all your orders and their current status. You will also receive WhatsApp updates directly from our team.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept Mobile Money (Orange Money, Africell Money) and Pay After Delivery. More payment options are coming soon.
Can I return a product?
Yes! Contact us within 24 hours of delivery via WhatsApp or email. Items must be unused and in their original condition.
How do I become a seller?
Tap "Sell on Zmafrdeal" in the menu. Register your store, add your products and start selling to thousands of customers.
How do I use a voucher code?
Enter your voucher code in the "Voucher Code" field on the checkout page before placing your order. The discount is applied instantly.
My order is wrong or damaged
Take photos and contact us immediately on WhatsApp with your order number. We will resolve it within 24 hours.
How do I delete my account?
Please message us on WhatsApp or email support@zmafrdeal.com with your request. We will process it within 3 business days.
