Seller Terms & Conditions

Seller Terms & Conditions

Last updated: June 2026. These terms govern your use of the Zmafrdeal Seller Portal.

1. Eligibility

You must be 18 years or older to register as a seller. By registering, you confirm that you have the legal right to sell the products you list. Zmafrdeal reserves the right to reject any application at its discretion.

2. Seller Responsibilities

  • Provide accurate and truthful product descriptions, photos, and pricing.
  • Maintain sufficient stock levels for all listed products.
  • Fulfill orders promptly and communicate clearly with buyers.
  • Keep your store information up to date, including contact details.
  • Respond to buyer inquiries within 48 hours of receiving them.

3. Commission & Payments

Zmafrdeal charges a 10% commission on every completed sale. This is deducted from your earnings before payout. You keep 90% of the sale price. There are no registration fees, no monthly fees, and no hidden charges. Payouts are processed on request to your registered mobile money or bank account.

4. Product Approval

All products are reviewed by our admin team within 24 hours of submission. Products that do not meet our quality standards, contain prohibited items, or have misleading descriptions will be rejected. Sellers will be notified of rejections with the reason provided.

5. Account Suspension

Zmafrdeal may suspend or permanently close a seller account for repeated policy violations, selling prohibited items, fraudulent activity, or receiving excessive buyer complaints. Sellers will be given a warning before suspension where possible.

6. Disputes

In the event of a dispute between a seller and buyer, Zmafrdeal will act as a neutral mediator. Sellers must cooperate with the dispute resolution process. Final decisions made by Zmafrdeal are binding.

Return & Refund Policy

Return & Refund Policy

Last updated: June 2026. This policy applies to all sellers and buyers on Zmafrdeal.

1. Return Window

Buyers may request a return within 7 days of receiving their order. Sellers may choose to extend this to up to 14 days, which should be stated clearly in the product description. After the return window, no returns are guaranteed.

2. Valid Reasons for Return

  • The item received is significantly different from the product description or photos.
  • The item arrived damaged or defective through no fault of the buyer.
  • The wrong item was delivered.
  • The item is missing key accessories or components as described.

3. Non-Returnable Items

  • Items damaged by the buyer after delivery.
  • Perishable goods (food, cosmetics opened by the buyer).
  • Digital products or downloads once accessed.
  • Customised or personalised items made to order.
  • Items without original packaging when required.

4. Return Process

  1. Buyer contacts the seller via WhatsApp or the messaging system.
  2. Seller reviews the return request and responds within 48 hours.
  3. If agreed, the seller arranges item collection or instructs the buyer to send it back.
  4. Once the item is received and inspected, the refund is processed within 3–5 business days.

5. Refund Method

Refunds are issued to the original payment method used by the buyer — either mobile money or credit/debit card. Shipping costs are refunded only if the return is due to seller error or a defective product.

6. Seller Obligations

Sellers must honour valid return requests. Repeatedly refusing valid returns will result in account warnings or suspension. Zmafrdeal may mediate in disputed returns and has the final say in resolution.

Commission Policy

Commission Policy

Last updated: June 2026. Zmafrdeal charges a simple, transparent commission on sales.

1. Commission Rate

Zmafrdeal charges a flat 10% commission on every successfully completed sale. This is the only fee you pay as a seller. There are no setup fees, no monthly subscription fees, and no listing fees.

2. When Commission is Charged

Commission is only deducted when a sale is marked as completed (order delivered and no return pending). Cancelled orders are not charged. If an order is refunded, the commission is reversed accordingly.

3. Payout Calculation

Your payout for each sale = Sale Price × 90%. For example, if a buyer pays Le 100,000, you receive Le 90,000 and Zmafrdeal retains Le 10,000 as commission. Shipping fees collected from buyers are passed to you in full if you arranged the delivery.

4. Payout Schedule

Payouts are processed on request. You can request a withdrawal from your seller dashboard at any time once your balance reaches the minimum threshold. Funds are transferred to your registered mobile money number or bank account within 1–3 business days.

5. Commission Adjustments

Zmafrdeal reserves the right to adjust commission rates with 30 days' notice. Any changes will be communicated via email and in-app notification. Existing confirmed orders at the time of change will be processed at the original rate.

Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

1. Information We Collect

  • Name, email address, phone number, and business information during registration.
  • Transaction data including orders, payments, and earnings.
  • Usage data such as pages visited and features used in your seller dashboard.
  • Device and browser information for security and fraud prevention.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate and improve the Zmafrdeal platform.
  • To process and fulfil orders and payments.
  • To send you notifications about orders and account activity.
  • To communicate important updates, policy changes, and support.
  • To detect and prevent fraudulent activity.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share your information only where necessary: with payment processors to process transactions, with buyers to fulfil their orders (name and contact for delivery), and with law enforcement if legally required.

4. Data Security

Your data is stored securely using industry-standard encryption. We use Supabase infrastructure with row-level security. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text. We regularly review our security practices.

5. Your Rights

  • Access your personal data at any time from your profile dashboard.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data by contacting support.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Opt out of marketing communications at any time.
Prohibited Items

Prohibited Items Policy

Last updated: June 2026. The following items are not permitted on Zmafrdeal under any circumstances.

Absolutely Prohibited

  • Weapons, firearms, ammunition, or explosive devices.
  • Illegal drugs, narcotics, or controlled substances.
  • Counterfeit or stolen goods of any kind.
  • Adult content or sexually explicit materials.
  • Human organs, body parts, or remains.
  • Animals (live or dead) without proper documentation.
  • Hazardous chemicals, poisons, or biological materials.
  • Items that infringe on intellectual property rights (fake branded goods).

Restricted Items (require approval)

  • Pharmaceutical products (must include proper licensing).
  • Alcohol and tobacco products (subject to regional regulations).
  • Second-hand electronics (must be clearly described as used).
  • Food products (must comply with health and safety standards).
  • Agricultural products (must meet import/export regulations).

Consequences of Violations

Listing prohibited items will result in immediate product removal, a warning to your account, and potentially permanent account suspension for repeat violations. Zmafrdeal cooperates with relevant authorities for serious violations including illegal goods. All decisions are final.

Reporting Violations

If you see a product listed on Zmafrdeal that you believe violates this policy, please contact our admin team immediately via WhatsApp or the seller support channel. We review all reports within 24 hours and take appropriate action.
